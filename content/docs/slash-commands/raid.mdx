# Team Presses

### **Elemental Overload**
- All elements get combined and there’s a guaranteed weakness effect
- **Power Surge**: Buffs ATK by 100% and Crit by 30% for 3 turns
- **Ultimate Combo**: Chain attack (damage x player count)
- **Divine Restoration**: Heals team to full HP
- **Za Warudo**: Adds 3 turns and freezes boss
- **Team Fortress**: Blocks 90% damage for 2 turns
- **Synchronized Assault**: Combines attack power of all players and hits the boss
- **Freeze**: Freezes the boss for two turns (no counter attack from it)

# Buffs / Effects

- **Adrenaline**: Buffs your ATK by 50%, gives you 40% lifesteal and gives you an extra turn - Free 50% ATK and 40% lifesteal? Sign me up. Use this. It’s also so convenient that it gives you an extra turn to use your ATK buff right away
- **Battle Cry**: Team gains 30% ATK and 20% Crit for 3 turns - Decent buffs, pretty solid, use if you want!
- **Time Warp**: Adds 3 extra turns and heals 15% HP - Use if you’re not confident in your ability to solo/party raid, really clutch when you’re running out of turns
- **Quantum Clone**: Makes 2-4 of clones of yourself that do damage, heal, or buff ATK - Incredibly useful, the heals and damage are so clutch especially when you get two that heal/hit, and the ATK buff is pretty good
- **Glitch**: Basically Reality Shatter but it doesn’t give everyone individual effects, going for giving you or the boss an effect, also with a side of gambling (ex. Making you lose 30% HP but the boss loses 30% too, freezing the boss for 2 turns, DOING 300% DAMAGE TO THE BOSS) - A little better than Reality Shatter, but more unstable because the effects are really diverse
- **Reality Shatter**: Gives an effect to every person that either: does damage to the boss (small damage), buffs player ATK or heals player by 30% (has a chance to buff the boss’ ATK by 25%, has a chance to stun the boss) - Use if you want to, the effects are pretty good but it’s not a must use
-# note it gives an effect to EVERY SINGLE PERSON
- **Rally**: Resets all team cooldowns, buffs teams ATK by 35%, heals team - Pretty good, nothing much to say, it’s just solid, use it if you want to
- **Elemental Overload**: Costs HP (amount taken depends on rank and tier), deals a lot of damage (around the same as counter or better), if it hits a weakness then bonus damage, gives 25% damage buff to the team - Effects are pretty good, 25% damage buff is nice but there are probably better moves for effects (this move is still really good btw)
