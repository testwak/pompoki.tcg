## About
This pack contains only standard cards, meaning **Hoyoverse game character-type cards**, with rarities ranging from 1★ to 5★. It features a pity system with early pity — the higher the number of pulls without a 5★, the greater your chances become. You are guaranteed a 5★ at a maximum of 160 pulls.
